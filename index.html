Ring Connection Test

Checks whether your iPhone can talk to your Oura ring. Read-only — it doesn't change anything on the ring.

Before you tap the button
  1. Open this page in the Bluefy app (not Safari).
  2. Force-close the Oura app (swipe it away).
  3. Have the ring on your finger or on its charger, near the phone.
